# Voice and copy

Words are design material. Generic SaaS vocabulary is a defect here, not a neutral default.

## Reference

Notion and Apple. Both write plainly, specifically, and like a person who respects you.
Neither uses hype. Neither explains what a button obviously does.

## Principles

**1. Say the specific thing.** "Sunday is gone" beats "save time on admin". "Priya hasn't
trained in 9 days" beats "client inactivity detected".

**2. Write from the user's side of the screen.** Name things as the person recognises them.
A coach has *clients*, not *end users*. They *send a plan*, they don't *provision an assignment*.

**3. Controls say what happens.** "Send plan" → toast: "Plan sent to Priya." Not "Submit"
→ "Success!"

**4. Errors explain and offer a way out.** What broke, and what to do. No apology, no
"Oops!", no "Something went wrong."
- Bad: "Error: request failed"
- Good: "Couldn't send — Priya's number isn't on WhatsApp. Add another number?"

**5. No exclamation marks.** Anywhere. Enthusiasm is conveyed by being useful.

**6. Sentence case everywhere.** Not Title Case For Interface Labels.

**7. Second person, active voice, present tense.** "Add a client", not "Clients can be added".

**8. Numbers are concrete.** "9 days", "3 of 12 sessions", "₹4,000/month" — not "a while",
"some", "affordable".

## Banned words and phrases

Using any of these is a review failure. They're the tell of generated copy.

> Dashboard · Manage your clients · Get started · Powerful · Seamless · Effortless ·
> Unlock · Elevate · Supercharge · Streamline · Revolutionise · Game-changing ·
> Take your coaching to the next level · All-in-one solution · Empower · Leverage (as a verb) ·
> Journey (for anything but an actual training arc) · Oops · Something went wrong ·
> We're sorry for the inconvenience · Click here · Learn more · Coming soon! · 🎉

**"Dashboard" specifically:** name the screen for what's on it. The coach's home is
*Today* — the clients who need them. Not a grid of stat tiles.

## Naming things

Prefer a specific word over a category word. "Duo" over "Flip". If a feature name could
sit on any competitor's product, it's the wrong name.

Current product vocabulary — use these exact terms, don't invent synonyms:

| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| coach | trainer, provider, professional |
| client | user, customer, trainee, athlete |
| plan | programme assignment, workout plan object |
| session | workout instance, log entry |
| check-in | survey, form, questionnaire |
| Today | Dashboard, Home, Overview |
| You | Profile, My Account, Settings (on the client app) |

Adding a term to this table is a product decision. Ask.
