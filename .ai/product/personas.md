# Personas

## Primary: the independent coach (the buyer)

**Who.** 24–38, certified, 5–50 paying clients, ₹3,000–8,000/client/month. Income
₹80,000–1,50,000/month if independent. Runs the business from a phone; uses a laptop
for programming.

**Technical sophistication.** Comfortable with Instagram, WhatsApp, UPI, Google Sheets.
Not comfortable with anything requiring setup, imports, or reading documentation.
Will abandon onboarding that takes more than one sitting.

**Their day.** Trains clients in person or on call, answers WhatsApp constantly, builds
programmes on Sunday, chases payments on the 3rd of the month.

**Frustrations, in the order they'd list them.**
1. Sunday is gone — hours duplicating spreadsheet tabs
2. Client messages have taken over their life; no boundary between 9pm and work
3. Clients quietly disappear and they find out at cancellation
4. Chasing money monthly, awkwardly, from people they also have to motivate
5. Can't grow past ~30 clients without quality collapsing

**What they actually want.** More clients, and to stop feeling like an admin. Not
"better software".

## Secondary: the client (decides retention)

**Who.** 22–45, urban India, budget-to-midrange Android, patchy signal in basement gyms.
Pays the coach ₹3,000–8,000/month. Lives in WhatsApp.

**Technical sophistication.** High for WhatsApp, Instagram and UPI. Low tolerance for a
new app, a new login, or anything they must learn.

**Frustrations.**
1. "This plan could have been sent to anyone"
2. Silence after paying — reads as being forgotten
3. Can't tell if it's working; the scale moves randomly
4. Fell behind, feels ashamed, now avoids the app entirely
5. The app crashed and ate their workout

**What makes them stay.** Visible progress, and feeling like a person rather than a form.

## Tertiary: the affiliate

Certification academies, gym equipment dealers, supplement distributors, coach-influencers,
and existing customers. Motivated far more by milestone lump sums than trickle commission.

## Internal: platform admin

You. Needs impersonation for support (audited), subscription visibility, and affiliate
payout reconciliation.
