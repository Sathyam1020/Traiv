# Product principles

Apply these when a decision isn't explicitly covered elsewhere.

## 1. The client's experience decides our revenue

The coach buys on coach-facing features and cancels because of client-facing ones.
When trading off, favour the client's side.

## 2. Don't fight WhatsApp

Clients live there. Workouts, nudges, check-in prompts and payment reminders go out
*over* WhatsApp as deep links into the app. The app is the depth layer, not the front door.

## 3. Never lose what someone typed

A logged set, a check-in answer, a food entry — written locally first, synced after,
idempotent on retry. Losing a logged set is the single most common complaint in this
category and it is upstream of all our churn.

## 4. Week one is the whole relationship

A client doing fewer than 3 workouts in 14 days churns at 3–4×. Anything that adds
friction to a client's first session is a revenue decision, not a UX detail.

## 5. Don't punish people for falling behind

Streaks reward the few who never break them and shame the many who do. Someone returning
after ten days sees "Welcome back. Here's today", not eleven red incomplete sessions.

## 6. Make the fast path and the personal path the same path

Coaches send generic plans because personalising is slow. So personalisation is
*built into* the fast action, not an extra step they'll skip.

## 7. Unlimited means unlimited

No client caps, no usage metering, no surprise tier walls.

## 8. Earn the right to build

Nothing gets built because a competitor has it. Features in v3 stay in v3 until v2
demonstrably retains.
