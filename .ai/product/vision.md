# Vision

## What Traiv is

A white-label coaching platform for independent coaches in India. The coach runs their
whole practice on it — programming, nutrition, check-ins, progress, messaging, payments.
Their clients get an app carrying the coach's name, logo and colour, not ours.

## Who it's for

**Primary user (the buyer):** independent coaches in India running a paying practice —
personal trainers, dietitians, nutritionists, yoga instructors, physiotherapists.
Typically 5–50 clients, charging ₹3,000–8,000 per client per month.

**Secondary user (the one who decides retention):** the coach's client. Does not pay us,
never sees our brand, and determines whether the coach stays subscribed.

**Explicitly not for:** gyms needing front-desk membership software. Different buyer,
different job.

## The core problem

Churn, and it runs as a chain. Each link causes the next:

1. Client signs up motivated; week one is thin, nobody walks them through it
2. Client stops logging → the coach goes blind, because dashboards only know what
   clients self-report
3. No visible results, plus shame about the gap, so they avoid the app entirely
4. Client cancels — the coach finds out last, three weeks after the signals appeared
5. The coach's income drops, and they cancel us

Nobody churns out of coaching software because a feature was missing. They churn
because their business shrank, or because the app made them look bad to the people
paying them.

## Current alternatives

- **WhatsApp + Google Sheets + a PDF diet chart.** Free, universal, already installed.
  This is the real competitor and most of the market uses it.
- **Trainerize, TrueCoach, Everfit, PT Distinction** — capable, per-client pricing,
  add-on stacking, USD billing.
- **FitBudd (Gurugram), Spur.fit** — Indian-built but USD-priced, $59–149/mo.
- **Fittr** — funded, but competes *with* coaches for clients rather than serving them.

## Why Traiv should exist

Every serious option bills Indian coaches in dollars at ₹5,000–12,000/month, prices
per client so growth is punished, and ships a food database full of foods Indians
don't eat. Nobody has built this for how coaching actually works here.

## Primary value proposition

**₹999/month, unlimited clients, delivered over WhatsApp, and it actually knows Indian food.**

Three defensible wedges:
1. **Price** — 5–11× cheaper than every USD-billed alternative, unlimited clients at
   every tier
2. **WhatsApp as the delivery layer** — we don't fight the channel clients already live
   in, we use it
3. **The Indian food database in home measures** — katori, roti, idli. Slow to build,
   which is exactly why it's a moat

## Positioning line

> Coach fifty people like you coach ten.

Never "AI writes your programs". A coach's deepest fear is being replaceable; we sell
leverage, not replacement.
