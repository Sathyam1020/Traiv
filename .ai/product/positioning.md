# Positioning

## Pricing (confirmed)

All prices **GST-inclusive** — Indian buyers expect the displayed price to be the
charged price. ₹999 inclusive nets ₹846.

| Tier | Monthly | Annual | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | — | 2 clients, forever |
| Starter | ₹499 | ₹4,990 | 8 clients |
| **Pro** | **₹999** | **₹9,990** | **Unlimited clients — the hero plan** |
| Studio | ₹2,499 | ₹24,990 | 5 coach seats, group features |

Annual = two months free. Push it hard: this category churns 5–13% monthly and an
annual contract is worth far more than the 17% given up.

Planned increases to ₹1,499 then ₹1,999. **Early adopters are grandfathered permanently
and told so** — that turns a price rise from a betrayal into a loyalty story. The schema
must carry `org_subscription.grandfathered_price_paise`.

## What never changes

- **Unlimited clients at every paid tier.** Never price per client. It's the single
  loudest complaint against TrueCoach, Everfit and Trainerize, and it's our headline.
- **No add-ons.** Nutrition, payments, automation, branding all included. Everfit
  charges separately for three of those; Trainerize charges $45/mo for nutrition.
- **No platform fee on coach revenue.** TrueCoach takes 5%. We take 0% — gateway cost only.
- **No branding setup fee.** Trainerize charges $169, FitBudd $75.
- **One-click cancel.** Immediate, no email, no retention chat. Cancellation friction
  affects 8 of 10 competitors; being the exception is worth advertising.
- **One-click full export.** "Leave any time, take everything." Converts switchers.

## Affiliate programme

40% for the first 12 months, then 15% for as long as the customer stays. Milestone cash
bonuses (₹2,000 at 5 active referrals, ₹6,000 at 15, ₹20,000 at 40). 60-day clawback.
Two paid months before any payout. Self-referral blocked by `UNIQUE(org_id)` on referral.

Not 40% forever — that leaves ~₹180–280 contribution against a ₹580–680 margin and
never recovers.

## Launch plan

First 8 design partners free, then 12 more. Selected for **already having 10+ paying
clients** — the thing being tested is whether *clients* use the app. Free is a trade:
weekly call, video testimonial, permission to publish their numbers.

## Go-to-market, in priority order

1. **Certification academies** (K11, INFS, Classic Fitness Group, NFNA) — they graduate
   batches of coaches with no incumbent tooling. Highest leverage, least worked.
2. **Instagram DMs**, manually, to coaches whose bio says "DM for coaching". The manual
   version is also the customer research.
3. **Free Indian food macro calculator** as a public tool — seeds the food database,
   ranks for searches no Western tool targets.
4. **Build in public.**
5. **Switcher campaign** — "moving from Trainerize or FitBudd? We'll migrate you free."
