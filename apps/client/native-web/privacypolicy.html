Health data in Traiv

With your permission, Traiv reads daily step totals and exercise sessions from your phone's health platform. These are stored in your coaching account and visible to your coach. Traiv requests read access only and does not write to Health Connect or Apple Health.

Disconnect in Profile → Integrations to stop future syncing. Revoke access at any time in your phone's health permissions. Existing imported history remains in your account; contact your coaching team for data deletion.
