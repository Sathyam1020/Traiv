# Start Traiv’s free video library carefully

**YMove’s 25 free MP4s are the clearest practical starting point for a commercial Traiv app; wger is the best candidate for expanding coverage after checking each asset.** No comprehensive, uniformly licensed, free commercial filmed-video database was verified. Several popular “free exercise databases” supply GIFs or images, restrict commercial use, or offer only evaluation access. A zero-license-fee catalog is feasible at modest scale, but storage, delivery and rights verification still take resources. This research recommends a limited initial catalog, not implementation. Sources were checked on **4 November 2026**. ([YMove](https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos), [wger video model](https://github.com/wger-project/wger/blob/master/wger/exercises/models/video.py))

## YMove supplies 25 usable filmed clips

YMove offers **25 downloadable 720p portrait MP4s**, comprising 11 studio clips and 14 filmed in a gym. Its page expressly permits use in apps, websites and client materials, requires no signup, and makes attribution optional. Standalone resale or redistribution as a competing video library is prohibited. The selection covers common movements including squats, bench press, deadlifts, rows, presses and machine exercises. That makes it useful for a core catalog, rather than comprehensive exercise coverage. ([Free library and license](https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos))

Download and app-use permission make this the strongest free option researched. Delivering the clips through Traiv’s own hosting is a reasonable reading of that grant, **not a separately verified CDN-specific permission**. Preserve the applicable terms with the source files. YMove’s larger advertised library is separately licensed; its existence does not expand the free sample’s rights. ([YMove](https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos))

## wger can expand coverage with asset checks

wger supports genuine uploaded video files, including MP4, MOV, WebM and Ogg, and provides commands to download exercise videos. Each video inherits a model recording its license, author and source. This supports selecting a locally maintained subset, but **the current video count and usable exercise coverage remain unverified** because the live service was inaccessible to the research client. A historical addition of 150 videos is not a current inventory. ([Video model](https://github.com/wger-project/wger/blob/master/wger/exercises/models/video.py), [download commands](https://wger.readthedocs.io/en/latest/administration/commands.html), [license fields](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wger-project/wger/master/wger/utils/models.py))

Do not apply wger’s software license indiscriminately to its media. Check each selected file’s exact license, attribution and provenance. CC BY-SA permits commercial redistribution, but requires credit, a license link and disclosure of changes; adaptations carry share-alike obligations. It does not itself guarantee publicity or privacy permissions. These media conditions do not automatically require independently written Traiv application code to be open-source. ([wger licensing](https://wger.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), [CC BY-SA terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/))

## Other free offerings miss essential requirements

**ExerciseDB’s free V1 supplies 180p GIFs, not actual videos**, and prohibits commercial products without a paid plan. Its newer repository’s code license does not override proprietary API-media terms, which prohibit persistent storage and bulk downloading. ([Free documentation](https://oss.exercisedb.dev/docs), [API terms](https://exercisedb.notion.site/ExerciseDB-API-Terms-of-Use-226983b728ca8090bf7be79564e4b356))

MuscleWiki has real MP4s, but its free tier is browser-Playground access rather than app integration. Paid API integration begins at $10/month for testing; videos must stream without downloading or rehosting, and streaming requests consume quota. ([FAQ](https://api.musclewiki.com/faq), [pricing](https://api.musclewiki.com/pricing), [API terms](https://api.musclewiki.com/api-terms))

The free-exercise-db repository supplies JPGs, and its maintainer explicitly cannot verify their origins or royalty-free status. VFE’s free animated MP4 pack excludes app/software assets. Programme permits commercial embedding but excludes compiling videos to replicate a similar or competing service, making a coaching-app library an unsuitable assumption without a scoped grant. ([Image provenance](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/issues/2), [VFE rules](https://vectorfitexercises.com/for-content-creators), [Programme license](https://programme.app/license))

## Conclusion

Traiv should choose its essential movements first, match YMove’s free clips, then evaluate wger assets for gaps. Keep a record of source, author, exact license, retrieval date and edits. Original coach-recorded demonstrations can fill remaining gaps. If broad, visually consistent coverage is mandatory immediately, the constraint that needs changing is the licensing budget; none of the verified free offerings establishes that complete solution.
