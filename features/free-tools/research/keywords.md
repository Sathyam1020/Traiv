# Phase 7a keyword research

Initial query inventory for all 109 calculators, generators, readers, and other tools. Templates is an existing page outside this inventory. Query ownership and promotion priority are editorial hypotheses. Search volume, difficulty, location-specific organic top ten, and People Also Ask still need collection. No measured ranking or keyword volume is implied.

## First search spot checks

- Coaching rates, income, and churn are distinct coach jobs. The [Coach+ tool directory](https://coachplusplatform.com/en/tools) lists a separate calculator for each. Start with these as candidates for coach-focused promotion. Benchmark results for each query individually before assigning difficulty.
- The [Bodyvault RPE calculator](https://bodyvault.app/tools/rpe-calculator) and [CalcBee RPE converter](https://calcbee.com/calculators/health/strength/rpe-percentage-converter-calculator/) both pair a converter with a percentage chart. Keep both queries on Traiv’s RPE page.
- [TDEE.co](https://tdee.co/) uses maintenance calories to explain TDEE and links to a separate macro calculator. Benchmark the TDEE and maintenance calorie queries together before changing either title. The working distinction is estimated expenditure versus choosing an intake target to maintain weight.
- Keep the historical churn rate calculator focused on measured loss over a period. Keep the churn risk reader focused on an individual client’s warning signs. Verify both queries separately before promoting them.
