# Scenarios

- Second attempt: importing identical observations and syncing twice does not duplicate leads or reset sales notes.
- Two actors: sequential edits with stale revisions reject rather than overwrite another salesperson.
- Out of order: older source evidence cannot replace newer evidence; Do Not Contact blocks new outreach logging.
- After expiry: old evidence appears stale and contributes no scoring points.
- Abandoned halfway: interrupted leased jobs can resume after lease expiry; completed jobs stay completed.
- Shared chain phone/domain never merges different branches; uncertain matches enter review.
- Unknown coaching/app data never earns unsupported points; score remains 0–100.
- Invalid contact/source/schema data is rejected; export cells cannot execute injected formulas.
- Sheets research refresh preserves notes, status, assignment and outreach history even when rows move.
