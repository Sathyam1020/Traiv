# Edge cases

Search snippets are discovery evidence, not proof of current trading. Public contact publication is not deliverability verification. A business website advertising Bengaluru service does not prove a coach lives there. No WhatsApp inference from a phone. Source disagreement stays visible; ambiguous entities stay separate pending review. Google Places data is not persisted or redistributed by default. Failed access is reported, never replaced with fake data. Formula capacity is explicit and expanded during export/sync.
